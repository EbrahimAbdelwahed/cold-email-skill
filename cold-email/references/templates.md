# Structures by Goal

These are skeletons, not copy-paste templates. Fill every bracket with specific, researched content, then rewrite until it sounds like the user. Never send a version where only the name changed.

---

## 1. Founder of a startup — join the team

**Subject:** Building with [Company] / [specific thing] at [Company]

- **Context:** one genuine observation about their product, feature, or problem.
- **Relevance:** the user's role + the most relevant project/stack for what they're building.
- **Proof:** one internship/client result/project with a link; portfolio + GitHub.
- **Ask:** "Are you currently open to bringing on [role]?"

Tip: the opening observation is what makes this feel personal — invest the research there.

---

## 2. Recruiter / hiring manager — a specific advertised role

**Subject:** [Role] at [Company] | [most relevant skill or project]

- **Context:** name the exact role (link it) and that you're reaching out directly.
- **Relevance:** the two skills from the job description the user matches best.
- **Proof:** one project or past experience with a specific contribution or result.
- **Ask:** "Are you still considering candidates for this position?"

Tip: read the job description first and mirror its priorities, not the user's whole stack.

---

## 3. Employee — asking for a referral

**Subject:** Quick question about [Role] at [Company]

- **Context:** the specific opening (with link) and that they work there.
- **Relevance:** the skill that matches the role.
- **Proof:** one relevant project/achievement + portfolio; attach resume here if appropriate.
- **Ask:** "If you think my profile fits, would you be comfortable referring me?"

Tip: give them everything they need to refer without searching (job link, resume, one-line summary). Check the role is still open.

---

## 4. Business owner — freelance client

**Subject:** An idea for [Business/Product]

- **Context:** a real, specific problem or improvement opportunity you noticed.
- **Relevance:** the service the user offers and how a concrete solution could help.
- **Proof:** similar past client work or a relevant example, with link.
- **Ask:** "Would you be open to me sharing a few ideas for [Business]?"

Tip: never invent problems or promise revenue gains you can't substantiate. A real observation plus a thoughtful suggestion is enough.

---

## 5. Short DM — LinkedIn or X

No subject. 2–4 short sentences.

- One specific observation about their work.
- The user's role + one relevant project or result.
- "Would love to connect and see if there's a fit." + one link.

Tip: if there's a connection-request character limit, cut further — start the conversation first, share work later.

---

## 6. Follow-up — no reply yet

**Subject:** Re: [original subject] (reply in the same thread)

- Reference the original message in one line.
- One short, genuine reason the user is still interested + one relevant skill/project.
- **Ask:** "Is this relevant for your team right now?"

Tip: send 3–5 business days after the original. One more later at most, then move on. Don't re-paste the whole pitch.

---

## Choosing

| Goal | Structure |
|------|-----------|
| Join a startup | 1 |
| Apply for an advertised role | 2 |
| Get a referral | 3 |
| Find freelance clients | 4 |
| Start a conversation on social | 5 |
| No reply after a few days | 6 |
| Something else | Use the formula directly |
